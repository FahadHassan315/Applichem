What We Do

Specialized building repair and protection services

Alongside homeowner waterproofing and heat proofing, Applichem delivers a wider set of specialized repair, strengthening, coating, anchoring, grouting, and cutting services for commercial and industrial needs.

01

Waterproofing

Roof, basement, bathroom, tank, and wall waterproofing systems designed to stop seepage, protect reinforcement, and reduce recurring repair costs.

02

Diamond Core Cutting

Clean, controlled openings through concrete and masonry for plumbing, HVAC, anchors, and utilities with minimal vibration and edge damage.

03

Rehabilitation

Planned restoration for aging or damaged structures, combining crack treatment, concrete repair, protection coatings, and strengthening where required.

04

Expansion Joints

Joint installation and sealing that allows structural movement while keeping water, dust, and debris from entering slabs, podiums, and roof areas.

05

Crack Injection

Epoxy and polyurethane injection for active and dormant cracks, helping restore integrity, block seepage, and slow further deterioration.

06

Concrete Repair

Repair of spalling, honeycombing, corrosion damage, and weak concrete using surface preparation, repair mortars, and protective finishes.

07

Re-Bar Anchoring

Chemical anchoring for reinforcement extensions, retrofits, and new connections where pull-out strength and accurate embedment matter.

08

Column Jacketing

Column strengthening with engineered jacketing methods to improve load capacity, restore damaged sections, and support renovation needs.

09

False Ceiling

Neat gypsum, grid, and acoustic ceiling installations for offices, homes, and commercial interiors with coordinated service access.

10

Sound Proofing

Acoustic treatments for rooms, offices, studios, and mechanical areas to reduce sound transfer and improve comfort inside the space.

11

Heat Proofing

Reflective and insulating roof treatments that reduce heat gain, improve indoor comfort, and support lower cooling load in hot seasons.

12

Epoxy Coating

Durable epoxy systems for floors and surfaces that need chemical resistance, easier cleaning, abrasion protection, and a professional finish.

13

Grouting

Cementitious and chemical grouting for void filling, base plate support, machinery foundations, leakage control, and structural gaps.

14

Building Jacketing

Building-level strengthening and protection for structures that need improved durability, load performance, or rehabilitation after distress.

15

Anchor Bolt Fixing

Accurate anchor bolt fixing for machines, steel members, brackets, and equipment foundations using suitable chemical or mechanical systems.

Our Process

A clear path from site issue to handover

We keep the work practical, documented, and easy to understand, so property owners and contractors know what is being done and why.

1

Site Assessment

We inspect the affected area, identify the root cause, and note site conditions before recommending any treatment.

2

Proposal

You receive a clear scope, suitable material system, timeline, and cost so decisions are made with confidence.

3

Execution

Our team prepares surfaces properly, applies the approved system, and supervises quality through the work.

4

Handover

We review the completed work, share care guidance, and close the job only after the agreed scope is complete.

Technical site knowledge matters when buildings face real-world conditions.

  • Material selection based on actual site exposure
