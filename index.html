What We Do

Our Specialized Services

From waterproofing to structural repair, we handle every aspect of building maintenance and restoration.

💧

Water Proofing

Comprehensive waterproofing systems for roofs, basements, and walls to prevent water ingress and structural damage.

🔩

Diamond Core Cutting

Precision core cutting for concrete, brick, and masonry using diamond-tipped equipment for clean, accurate results.

🏗️

Rehabilitation Work

Full structural rehabilitation and restoration of deteriorated buildings, restoring integrity and extending lifespan.

🔧

Expansion Joint

Installation and sealing of expansion joints to accommodate thermal movement and prevent cracking in structures.

💉

Crack Injection

Epoxy and polyurethane crack injection to repair structural and non-structural cracks in concrete and masonry.

🧱

Concrete Repair

Spalling, carbonation, and corrosion-related concrete repair using high-performance repair mortars and coatings.

⚙️

Re-Bar Anchoring

Chemical and mechanical anchoring of reinforcing bars into existing concrete structures for added strength.

🏛️

Column Jacketing

Structural strengthening of existing columns using concrete or fiber-reinforced polymer (FRP) jacketing systems.

🪨

False Ceiling

Design and installation of false ceiling systems including grid, gypsum board, and acoustic tile configurations.

🔇

Sound Proofing

Acoustic insulation solutions for offices, residential spaces, and industrial buildings to reduce noise transmission.

☀️

Heat Proofing

Reflective and insulating heat proofing treatments for roofs and walls to reduce indoor temperatures.

🛡️

Epoxy Coating

Heavy-duty epoxy floor and surface coatings for industrial, commercial, and residential applications.

🪣

Grouting

Cementitious and chemical grouting for filling voids, stabilizing foundations, and waterproofing joints.

🏢

Building Jacketing

Full-building structural jacketing to upgrade seismic resistance and load-bearing capacity of existing structures.

⚓

Anchor Bolt Fixing

Precision installation of anchor bolts and fasteners for machinery, equipment, and structural connections.
